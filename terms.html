WonderScope Publisher — Effective November 3, 2026 (version 2026-11-03)

1. About the Tool

WonderScope Publisher (the "Tool") is a private command-line tool that uploads short videos to the operator's own YouTube channels through YouTube API Services. Its developer operates it for the developer's own channels, and it is not offered to the public. The Tool is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google.

2. YouTube Terms of Service

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the Tool, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

3. Privacy

The Privacy Policy explains what information the Tool accesses and how it uses, stores, shares and deletes that information. You must agree to it before you authorize the Tool. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

4. Your content

You are responsible for the videos and information that you upload with the Tool. Before you approve an upload, make sure that the content complies with the YouTube Terms of Service (including the YouTube Community Guidelines), and do not violate others' copyright or privacy rights.

5. Uploads need your approval

The Tool uploads a video only after you approve it. When you approve, you choose the title, the description, the privacy setting (public, unlisted or private), whether the video is made for kids, and the time. The Tool shows the exact information that it will send to YouTube, and it sends what you approved without changes.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the Tool's access at any time with pnpm oauth:revoke --channel <id> or at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. The Privacy Policy explains what happens to the stored information.

7. No warranty

The Tool is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. To the extent permitted by law, the developer is not liable for any loss arising from the use of the Tool.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how information is handled, the Tool asks you to agree again.
